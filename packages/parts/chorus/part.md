# The chorus counts

Where the song says what it is. Its first line is not the mind's to write: it is how long the song has been going, and grows by a bar at a time on a single reciting note, as psalms were sung by people with more words than tune. Its second line rhymes with that age, and the rest are varied lightly from the chorus before, so the same words come back and are never quite the same. It ends at home.
