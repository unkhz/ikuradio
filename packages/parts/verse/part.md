# The verse tells

Where the song tells its story. The mind writes it four lines at a time, from a memory of its own or one it heard from the robot, always from the verse that stood in this place before. It is sung to the tune's verse phrases, and each line is counted from its phrase and the wordiness it is told: sparse, with room for long vowels, or as full as the phrase can carry. It ends open, on the fifth, as a thing that is not finished.
