# The Triptych

A progressive three-movement suite separated by deliberate silences, using all six Tracks selectively so each movement inhabits a completely different sonic world.

- **Movement I — Earth (64 bpm):** An eight-bar `interlude`, solo soft `verse-1`, and duet `pre-chorus` carried by cavernous half-time `ritual` drums (`drums:mov1`), deep sub-octave `drone:mov1`, and dark MusicGen beds.
- **Intermission I (`pause-1`, 4 s `silence`)** clears the air completely.
- **Movement II — Air (free time → 84 bpm):** Dry free-time `spoken` poetry (`mov2-spoken`, no bed) and a 14-second free-time `bridge` hover under a luminous upper-octave `drone:mov2` (`octave: 1`), before a delicate swung `pulse:mov2` enters at 84 bpm under a soft duet `refrain`.
- **Intermission II (`pause-2`, 3 s `silence`)** holds a breath before the finale.
- **Movement III — Fire (92 bpm → 60 bpm):** Pressed choir `pre-chorus` (`mov3-build`), an eight-bar instrumental `surge`, and a full-throttle `chorus` drive forward with `tribal` drums (`drums:mov3`) and radiant mid-register `drone:mov3`, before dropping to 60 bpm — drums gone — for a vast slow-motion `outro` floating over cooling sub-octave drone embers. Every bed has a matching two-voice guide.
