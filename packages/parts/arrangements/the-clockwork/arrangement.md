# The Clockwork

A brisk, kinetic, minimalist motorik piece built around the pitched modal `pulse` Track and tight, glassy voices — with no drone at all.

Locked initially to 102 bpm, ten bars of dry `interlude` (`tick-in`) establish a zero-swing pitched `pulse:gear-1` before a precise solo `verse-1` (`humanness: 0.35`), a pressed duet `pre-1`, and an open first `chorus` (`ending: half`). After a six-bar mechanical `pivot`, the whole piece shifts up a gear to 114 bpm: denser `pulse:gear-2` and driving zero-swing slow-trance `drums:motorik` ignite through `verse-2`, a twelve-bar instrumental `sprint`, and the second `chorus` (`ending: full`). Drums drop out for a tight echoing `refrain` (`echo-1`) over pulse alone, before a sudden downshift to 84 bpm (`drift-out` and a soft solo `wind-down` refrain) lets the clockwork unwind gently home. Every bed has a matching two-voice guide.
