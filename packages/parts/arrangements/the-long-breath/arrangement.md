# The Long Breath

The other side of the coin from the Song as it always was: one unbroken arc of eight regions, with no turn, instead of two symmetrical passes at a single pulse. It opens hushed and sparse (`verse-1`, low ceiling, still) with a bed that rises gently out of silence and crossfades directly into `verse-2` without a bridge. After a single four-bar breath (`bridge-1`), the first `chorus` ends open on the fifth rather than coming home. Then the pulse dissolves altogether for twelve seconds of free time (`bridge-2`, ambient bed fading in). On the other side it wakes at 96 bpm — `verse-3` dense and lifted, then the second `chorus` with its own 96 bpm keys, still leaning open — before settling back to the Mood's own tempo for a sparse `final-chorus` that finally comes home and exhales over a five-second tail. Every region has its own two-voice guide so every bed listens to the music beneath it.
