# The Song as it always was

A verse, a chorus, a verse, a chorus, a verse, and a last verse that the song has always called its final chorus. The kinds are crossed by a bridge wherever one meets another, and a verse that follows a verse meets it plainly, which is why there is no bridge before the last. Ten parts in all, written out here instead of made by a rule: the chorus is one instance heard twice, and so sings the same words twice.
