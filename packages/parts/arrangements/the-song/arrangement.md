# The Song as it always was

An iteration is the whole order walked once: a verse, a chorus, a verse, a chorus, a verse, and a last verse that the song has always called its final chorus, with a bridge wherever one kind meets another. The Song lists the ten names twice, and the words turn over at the second hearing of the first verse: a new iteration begins there, the Song's age moves a number, and each name goes on with its own words from before the turn. The final chorus says its ending is full, so both iterations come home as the Studio makes them. On the timeline, a voice sings each region: the words of a region that has them, and, under a bridge, a hum that is aloud or not as each Song is made. A guide is hummed under every region, from its own words, and a bed is grown from it, longer than its region by three seconds and fading under the next. The chorus is one name heard twice in an iteration, so it is one sound: one voice, one guide and one bed, each placed twice; every other region is a sound of its own. Whether a bed is grown anew or taken from the shelf, to be played again, is the Studio's to decide as it makes the Song. The keys of the second iteration say so, since a turn changes no key by itself.
