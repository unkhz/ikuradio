# The Song as it always was

An iteration is the whole order walked once: a verse, a chorus, a verse, a chorus, a verse, and a last verse that the song has always called its final chorus, with a bridge wherever one kind meets another. The Song walks it twice, a pass at a time, and the second pass sings new words that follow the first's, each region continuing the one of the same place. On the timeline, a voice sings each region that has words, a guide is hummed under every region, and a bed is grown from it, longer than its region by three seconds and fading under the next. The chorus is one instance heard twice in a pass, and so its voice is one sound, placed twice; a bed is one for each kind of part in a pass, and so is shared by all the verses of it.
