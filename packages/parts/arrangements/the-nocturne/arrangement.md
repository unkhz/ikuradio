# The Nocturne

A hushed, late-night chamber piece of spoken poetry, real silence, and breathing drones — with no drums, no pulse, and no chorus.

It opens in stillness (`stillness`, 2.5 s) as a high-register pad `drone` (`octave: 1`, blooming softly from `0.22` to `0.65`) rises out of dark air. A dry free-time `spoken` prelude enters over the drone alone, followed by a sparse solo `verse-1` at 62 bpm with a fragile low-gain MusicGen bed. After three seconds of open `silence` (`breath-1`) and a six-bar nocturnal `interlude` (`drift`), the high drone gives way to a deeper sub-octave `drone:deep` (`octave: -1`) under a longer three-line free-time `spoken` reflection. A soft duet `verse-2` at 60 bpm, one last silent breath (`breath-2`), and a lone solo echoing `refrain` settle home over a long fading tail. Every bed has its own matching two-voice guide.
