# The Ascent

A single unbroken five-to-six-minute climb with no turn, built around one long evolving buildup, a cliff-edge drop into silence, one massive cathartic chorus, and a glacial slow-motion release.

It begins in haze: twelve bars of spacious instrumental `interlude` (`opening-haze`) as a subterranean sub-octave `drone` (`warmth: 0.12 → build: 0.92`) and distant tribal frame-drum heartbeat (`drums:ascent`, `intensity: 0.12 → build: 0.96`) slowly emerge. An intimate dry `spoken` invocation (`whisper-1`, no bed) enters over the dark pulse, followed by a hushed solo `verse-1`. From there, long evolving `interlude` waves (`drift-1`, `drift-2`) alternate with two intensifying `pre-chorus` climbs — voices growing from solo to duet to pressed full choir as syncopated toms, wooden rim clicks, breathing shakers, and rolling pre-drop tension crest together.

At the summit edge, everything cuts dead for 3.5 seconds of `silence` (`hold-breath`). Then the Song's one and only `chorus` detonates in full pressed choir, driving slow-trance percussion (`drums:peak`), radiant full-register `drone:peak`, and lush band. On the far side, the tempo drops to 56 bpm for a dramatic three-line slow-motion `outro` (`finale`, stretched double-length sustain) carried by cavernous half-time `ritual` drums receding into dust (`intensity: 0.55 → 0.06`) and a sub-octave drone cooling into embers, before an eight-bar `afterglow` and a lone solo one-line `refrain` (`coda`) settle home. Every instrumental and vocal bed has its own matching two-voice guide.
