# Tempo turns

One pass, and what the Song cannot do: a bridge in free time, with no beat, hummed for ten seconds; a chorus after it at another tempo; the first tempo back for the last verse. And one bed that runs under two regions, the verse and the bridge that follows it, instead of one bed to a part.
