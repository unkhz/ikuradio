# Mood

The weather a Song is sung in: its mode and its tempo, and the words that describe it.

A Mood holds a character, which says the mode the Tune is sung in and how fast, and its weather, a few descriptions the words are written in. It drifts a little with every Song: one description lets go, and the song chooses the one that arrives, from the last words it sang.
