# Tides

The Settings drift slowly, over many Songs, each by an envelope of its own.

A Tide moves one numeric Setting of one part, rising and falling over several Songs: the verse's melody climbs and settles, the chorus swells. The Tides are applied before the Perspectives' Turns.
