# Perspectives

The ways a Song may be heard differently from the usual. Facets make the Settings.

Each Perspective turns one Facet of the Settings for one Song: how the choir sings, how the bridges are hummed, how wordy a verse is. A Song is given a few of them when it begins, drawn by wrongness, and the Settings its hooks follow are the defaults with their Turns on top.
