# Tonicized verses

The verses lean temporarily into a secondary tonal center — relative major, chromatic mediant, subdominant, dominant, flat sixth, or Neapolitan — pivoted by an explicit leading-tone or half-step approach interval in the guide so the band follows the temporary key before returning home.
