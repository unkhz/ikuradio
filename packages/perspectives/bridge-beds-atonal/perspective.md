# Atonal bridges

The bridges' beds are atonal, for the whole Song, because their guides are. The Music is an old model that only grows from the voice it hears: told in words to be atonal, it grows anything, at any tempo, unrelated to the Song. So the guide hum keeps the bridge's rhythm, in the Song's tempo, and the shape of its rise and fall, and every note takes the next pitch of a row of all twelve, drawn for the Song, so no key can settle. The bed follows that hum, with the Mood's own band.
