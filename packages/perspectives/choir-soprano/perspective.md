# A soprano

The lead sings as it always does, and the first voice of the choir, a woman or a child, never a man, stands about an octave above it, where the harmonizer finds it room: within what that voice can still sing as a voice, a sixth or a fifth above where the octave would climb past it. The next voice stands below, as a choir does.
