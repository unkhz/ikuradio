# Walking verses

The verses walk a classic progression, chosen as a variant: i–VII–VI–V, i–VI–III–VII, ii–V–I, or round the circle of fifths. The roots are degrees of the Song's own mode, so each takes on its colours; the departure and the half close stay the verse's own.
