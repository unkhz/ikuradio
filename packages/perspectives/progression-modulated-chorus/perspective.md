# Modulated chorus

The chorus lifts or shifts into a new key — a half step, whole step, minor third, chromatic mediant, fourth, or warm whole-step drop — while the preceding verse or bridge ends on a key-defining pivot interval so the band hears the modulation and locks into the new tonal center.
