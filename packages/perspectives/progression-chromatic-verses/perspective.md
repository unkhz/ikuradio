# Chromatic verses

The verses travel through semitones instead of circle-of-fifths jumps, chosen as a variant: chromatic approach chords resolving by half step (including bII to I), a descending chromatic lament, a rising chromatic climb, or a twelve-tone walk where +/- 1 semitone is the cheapest move.
