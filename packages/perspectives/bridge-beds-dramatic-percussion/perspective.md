# Dramatic percussion on the bridges

The bridges' beds are only percussion, one kind for the whole Song, chosen from `prompts.json`: war drums, spanish clapping, church bells and the like. They are told the Song's tempo, but not the Mood's band, and they do not follow the guide's melody.
