# Dramatic percussion on the bridges

The bridges' beds are only percussion, one kind for the whole Song, chosen from `prompts.json`: war drums, spanish clapping, church bells and the like. They are told the Song's tempo, but not the Mood's band, and they follow the guide hum like every other bed: the hum is what keeps them in the Song's rhythm. Only the words change what is played.
