# Finnish vocals

The Song is written and sung in Finnish: the Studio wakes Poro (`models/poro.gguf`) for its mind instead of the usual English model, writes Finnish verses, choruses, title, and thought in the song's own spirit, and sings every line through Chimera's vocal tract using deterministic Finnish IPA syllables — unaspirated stops, consolidated geminates, and balanced Finnish opening diphthongs. The choir, melody, guide, and band work as usual; when the next English Song begins, the English lyric lineage picks up where it left off.
