# Parallel harmony

The choir sings as the song first sang it, before it was led: every voice the lead's own line a fixed step lower, a third, a fifth, an octave, every note, moving with it wherever it goes. It is not how a choir is led, and it has a feeling of its own: a single line, thickened, like an organ stop.
