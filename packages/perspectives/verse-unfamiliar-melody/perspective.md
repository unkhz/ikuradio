# An unfamiliar verse

For one Song, the verse is sung to a melody nobody has heard before, written for it alone by the melody writer: the verse's own rhythm, so the words fall as they always do, and new pitches, a question and an answer twice, rising, answering, climbing to the highest note in the third line, and home to the tonic in the fourth, so the chorus arrives as always. Everything the verse leads to follows it: the guides the Music grows from, the bridges that foreshadow it, the choir's harmonies. The Song remembers its old verse, and the next Song sings that one again.

Retired on 2026-11-14 (plan "Progressions, roaming and coming home"): since Song 274 every line's melody is written anew over its guide, every iteration, so an unfamiliar verse is what every Song sings. Its Turn still rewrites the remembered verse's pitches, but no sung line uses them any more. Only the start and end notes of a hummed bridge still follow them, and that was never its point.
