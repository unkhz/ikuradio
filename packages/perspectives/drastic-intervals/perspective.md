# Drastic intervals

The verses and the bridges keep their rhythm, but every change of note leaps to a note of the key, between a least and a most drawn for the Song: the least from three whole tones to five, the most from five to twelve, up or down as the lead's voice has room to, so that near the top of its range it leaps down and near the bottom up. The choir finds its harmonies beside the leaps by the same rules as always. The chorus is the anchor, and is sung as always. The bridges' guide hums the same leaps, so the Music follows them.
