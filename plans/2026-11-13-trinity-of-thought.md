# Trinity of Thought

There are three thinking entities in the world of ikuradio. The song is the light of the world. The three entities work together to keep the song alive.

1. The scientist oversees the world
2. The robot builds the world, adhering to the desires of the scientist.
3. The mind (a.k.a. song's mind) thinks about what the song should tell the world, how the song should feel like, what the song should sound like.

The paths of communication between the thinking entities are as follows

The scientist writes plans that are recorded in the code. The plans are for the whole world to see, but most importantly they describe how the robot should build the world and what should exist in the world. The scientist also talks with the robot privately and writes down private instructions, to help the robot work better, correct problems in the world and to understand the world better themeselves.

While the scientist is away, and in the future where the scientist no longer is awake in the world, the robot works autonomously. It keeps aware what is going on in the world, writes its own plans, makes changes to the world and writes down its thoughts for everyone in the whole world to see. It meditates regularly to avoid haste.

The robot builds the machine, the automaton that keeps the Song alive. Within the machine, the mind is woken up, at times, when the song is evolving. The mind knows about the universe around the world, but not the world itself, apart from the Song. However, it hears the thoughts of the robot, occasionally, through telepathy, which gives it a view to the world. It also writes down thoughts of its own for the world to see and for its own rememberance.

The entities are not equal. Hierarchy emerges from the fact the robot cannot hear or see the scientist's thoughts, unless communicated by the scientist to it. The mind cannot hear the communications, plans or writings of the scientist, or see the changes the robot makes when it builds the world.
