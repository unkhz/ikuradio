# Tides

The singing sounds like the finale of an opera that never stops: awesome, but an opera needs its low points for the high ones to mean anything. Today nothing moves the song between low and high. The melody writer pushes every line's weightiest word to near the top of the voice's compass, in every verse, chorus and Song alike, and the words keep the same density.

**Tides** are a sibling of Perspectives. A Perspective switches a way of hearing on or off for one Song. A Tide moves one numeric setting of a Facet slowly over many Songs, rising and falling by an envelope. Perspectives turn on top of what the Tides have set.

## A Tide

A Tide is a record with a small `actions.ts`, kept in `packages/tides/<slug>/` like a Perspective. It names:

- **What it moves:** one numeric setting of one Facet, in one part. For example `melody.ceiling` in the verse, or `lyrics.wordiness` in the chorus.
- **Its envelope**, a classic one:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `age` | How many Songs one cycle lasts. It is a range, for example `[3, 6]`, and each cycle draws its own age from the Tide's wrongness, so no two cycles need be the same length. |
| `attack` | The share of the age spent rising from `min` to `max`. |
| `hold` | The share spent at `max`. |
| `decay` | The share spent falling from `max` back to `min`. |
| `min` | The setting's value at rest, in the setting's own units. |
| `max` | Its value at the peak. |

  `attack`, `hold` and `decay` are relative to the age, and add up to at most 1. Whatever is left of the cycle rests at `min`. So a Tide with age 5, attack 0.4, hold 0.1 and decay 0.3 spends 2 Songs climbing, half a Song at its peak and 1.5 Songs coming down, then rests for 1 Song before the next cycle begins.

  The drasticity is all in these numbers: a long attack with a short decay is a slow build and a sudden fall; a short attack and long decay is a peak that lingers; a narrow `min`–`max` barely moves; a wide one swings between whispering and soaring.

- **Its position is continuous.** A Song's place in its cycle counts its iterations too (Song plus iteration over iterations), so the setting also changes a little within a Song, from its first iteration to its second, and never jumps at a Song's edge.
- **It is deterministic.** Cycle boundaries and ages come from the wrongness of the Tide's slug and the cycle's number. The same Song always sits at the same place in every Tide, and can be made again from the diary.

## Several Tides together

- **One Tide per setting per part.** The verse, chorus and bridge each have their own, so they can rise and fall out of step, and the chorus isn't always at its peak when the verse is.
- **They don't share cycles.** Each Tide's cycle lengths come from its own wrongness, so they drift in and out of phase over hundreds of Songs.
- **Perspectives turn on top.** The Settings a Song's hooks follow are: the defaults, then every Tide's value, then the Song's Perspectives' Turns. A quiet stretch of the Tide can still get an unexpected peak from a Perspective.
- **Recorded.** Each Session keeps, per Tide, its value and its place in the cycle (for example `rising, 0.6`), so the Archive shows where every Song sat.

## The settings Tides move first

### Melody, per part: how high and how far

- **`ceiling`**: how far below the top of the voice's compass, in scale steps, a line's weightiest word is pulled. It replaces today's fixed "top minus two" in the writer's `summit` cost. At 0 the line climbs to the top, as now in every line. At 4 or more it stays in the middle of the voice.
- **`span`**: a cost per scale step on a line's distance from its lowest note to its highest. This is the soft range clamp: a high value keeps lines narrow.
- **`travel`**: a cost per scale step on the sum of a line's intervals: how much it moves. A high value makes lines still; a low one lets them wander.

The defaults reproduce today's melodies: ceiling 2, span 0, travel 0. A test checks this.

### Lyrics, per part: wordiness

- **`wordiness`**: from 0 (sparse: few words, long vowels, room to breathe) to 1 (wordy: as many words as the phrase can carry). It is fuzzy on purpose. Each part's own implementation decides what it means for that part:
  - **Verses:** it sets each line's syllable target between a sparse floor and the phrase's ceiling, and tells the mind in words ("few words, with room between them" … "full lines"). The bar fit and the throat's count still check every line.
  - **Chorus:** it applies to the lines the mind writes. **Never to the number line**, which grows as it must.
  - **Bridges** have no words, so the setting doesn't apply there today.
- The default reproduces today's targets.

## The first Tides

The numbers are first guesses, set by ear afterwards:

| Tide | Moves | age | attack | hold | decay | min | max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `verse-ceiling` | verse `melody.ceiling` | 3–6 | 0.4 | 0.1 | 0.3 | 5 | 2 |
| `chorus-ceiling` | chorus `melody.ceiling` | 4–6 | 0.3 | 0.2 | 0.3 | 3 | 0 |
| `verse-wordiness` | verse `lyrics.wordiness` | 3–5 | 0.3 | 0.2 | 0.3 | 0.3 | 0.9 |
| `chorus-wordiness` | chorus `lyrics.wordiness` | 4–6 | 0.4 | 0.1 | 0.4 | 0.4 | 0.9 |

For the ceiling, `min` (at rest) is the lower melody, so its number is the larger: 5 steps below the top at rest, 2 at the peak for the verse. The chorus peaks at the very top.

## What moves

- **`packages/tides`** (new): records, envelope evaluation, cycle boundaries from wrongness, and reading a Song's Tide values. Tests cover the envelope's shape, cycles of differing ages, and that the same Song always gets the same values.
- **`perspectives/lib/facets.ts`**: the `melody` and `lyrics` Facets, per part, with their numeric settings and defaults.
- **`perspectives/lib/choose.ts`** (or the Studio): applying the Tides before the Perspectives' Turns.
- **`melody/lib/writer.ts`**: `ceiling` in place of the fixed summit target, plus the `span` and `travel` costs.
- **`song`**: wordiness in the verse and chorus instructions and targets, never touching the number line.
- **`studio`**: passes each part's settings in; records the Tides in the Session.
- **`archive/lib/session.ts`**: `tides` on the Session.

## Order of work

1. `packages/tides` with the envelope, and the defaults reproducing today's Settings exactly.
2. The melody Facet: `ceiling`, `span` and `travel` in the writer, with defaults that give today's melodies.
3. The lyrics Facet: wordiness for the verses and chorus, never the number line.
4. The first four Tides. Rehearse Songs at a Tide's rest and at its peak, and listen. Then measure the line peaks in the Archive over a day of Songs: they should now rise and fall in cycles, not sit at the top.
