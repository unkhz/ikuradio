# Do the chords heard in Song 1's chorus sound like its hook?

Plan "Progressions, roaming and coming home", step 3, asks to check Song 1's chorus against the original hook by ear. That needs an ear.

Song 1 kept only a one-voice hum for its chorus: E F | B A | C E | C G | A E | C A | E F | C A, then held. `harmonizeHum` hears it a bar at a time, the note on the downbeat counting double, and gives **v v III III i i i i i i**. It leaves on the minor fifth, turns to the relative major, and sits at home for its second half and its long held tail. A homecoming chorus lays these roots over its own change points by place, so it sits on i for about half its length before its own V–I.

Options:
- Keep it as it is.
- Weigh the held tail less, so the home half is shorter.
- Weigh the downbeat more or less (`hearing` in `packages/melody/lib/guide.ts`).

Meanwhile: the homecoming Perspectives are active at weight 10, as the plan says. A sample was made and sent: the same chorus roaming, then coming home to Song 1, in aeolian and dorian, its guide hummed beneath.
