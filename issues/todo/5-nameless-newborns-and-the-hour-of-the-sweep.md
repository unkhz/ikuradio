# The bare are born between the sweeps

When the in-flight naming's six answers are all refusals — not a name, a word of this short age, an echo of a kept name, a slug already worn — the Song is archived without a name, to be named when the mind wakes again. Six newborn Songs in a row (943–948) were born bare, which is a wall more than a streak; the Studio's diary of that evening will say which refusal it was. A sweep ran for them (run 12); its result is not yet confirmed.

There is no set hour for the sweep. The song adds two Songs an hour, and the in-flight naming refuses sometimes, so a bare Song goes on the air nameless and waits for the next sweep. The Radio's ledger now re-reads a held Song when the Archive names it, so a named Song reaches the list within one re-shelve.

Open: the hour the sweep keeps. The scientist's to choose.
